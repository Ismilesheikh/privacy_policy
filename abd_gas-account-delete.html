Delete Your Account

App Name: ABD Gas Data Management

Developer: Ismile Sheikh

How to delete your account

  1. Open the app
  2. Go to the menu (Drawer)
  3. Click on your name/profile
  4. Open your profile page
  5. Tap on "Delete Account"

What happens when you delete your account

When you request account deletion:

  • Your account will be permanently deleted
  • All your data stored in Firebase Authentication will be removed
  • All your data stored in Firebase Firestore Database will be deleted

Data retention

No personal data is retained after account deletion. All data is permanently erased from our systems.
