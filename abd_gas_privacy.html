Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2024

1. Introduction

Welcome to ABD Gas Data Management. Your privacy is important to us. This policy describes how we handle the data you input into our application.

2. Data Collection

The app collects and stores the following information:

  • Email address for secure account login.
  • Customer names, phone numbers, and IDs.
  • Delivery status and DAC numbers.
  • Location names for record organization.

3. Data Usage

Information is used solely to provide gas delivery tracking, generating PDF reports, and showing historical booking dates for your customers.

4. Security

We use Firebase Security Rules to ensure your data is private. Your customer records are linked to your specific account and cannot be viewed by other unauthorized users.

5. Third-Party Services

This app utilizes Google Firebase for database management and authentication. Google may collect diagnostic data as part of their standard service.
